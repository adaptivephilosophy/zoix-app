Zoix support

Zoix Privacy policy zoix.ai

Zoix is the free iOS app of the Adaptive Philosophy Foundation, a Washington nonprofit corporation. Home shows zoix.ai. Updates lists the foundation's public articles. Mot du jour shows a French word and its English meaning.

Contact

Email DanBoice@adaptivephilosophy.foundation.

The repository is public. You can also open a GitHub issue.

Giving

The Donate button opens the foundation's giving page in Safari, outside the app. Zoix does not see card numbers, does not process payments, and does not issue tax receipts.
